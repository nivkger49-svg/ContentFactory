# Hook And Script Tracking

## Why

Hooks, scripts, CTAs, and creative formats are reusable assets. They should be stored as versions, tested, scored, promoted, or retired.

## Statuses

Use these statuses consistently:

- `backlog` — promising but not tested yet.
- `testing` — currently used in organic/UBT tests.
- `winner` — strong signal; make variants and consider ads.
- `scale` — actively turning into more variants and paid tests.
- `watch` — mixed result; needs another test or different visual.
- `retire` — do not use again unless a clear reason appears.

## Core Tables

- `hooks`: first-line ideas, pain/angle/funnel fit, score, status.
- `scripts`: full voiceover/subtitle scripts connected to hooks.
- `creative_variants`: exact production recipe: hook + script + funnel + visual style + voice style.
- `hook_evaluations`: performance records from Reels/FB/Instagram/TikTok/ads.

## Ranking Logic

A hook should not be judged by views alone. Rank by a blended signal:

- early hold / 3-second retention,
- average watch time,
- completion rate,
- saves/comments/shares,
- clicks,
- leads/purchases,
- consistency across variants.

## Decision Rules

- High views but no clicks: keep as organic/awareness, test a clearer CTA.
- Good hold but bad completion: script or pacing problem, not necessarily hook problem.
- Bad first 3 seconds across variants: retire or rewrite hook.
- Good result in one visual only: tag as visual-dependent, do not generalize too fast.
- Winner across 2+ variants: make 5 more variations and consider paid.

## Future APIs

When ElevenLabs/DALL-E/Veo APIs are added, generated outputs should be linked back to script/hook/variant ids.
