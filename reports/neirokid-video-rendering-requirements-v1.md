# NeiroKid Video Rendering Requirements v1

## Main Video Goal

The video must not just explain.

The video must hold a mom's attention in the first 3 seconds and strengthen the voiceover.

Every scene must visually confirm the spoken words.

## Overall Structure

Duration:

- 35-60 seconds

Format:

- 9:16

Tempo:

- fast
- dynamic
- shot change every 1-3 seconds

## First 3 Seconds

Required.

The frame must show:

- a problem, or
- a strong emotion, or
- a conflict

Forbidden:

- pretty neutral footage
- a child simply playing
- abstract intro cards

Preferred opening situations:

- meltdown
- tears
- exhausted mom
- child not listening
- overstimulated child

## Strong Visual Hook Rule

A strong visual hook means the first 3-5 seconds immediately make the viewer think:

`О, це про мою дитину`

The opening should not merely look nice.

It should feel impossible to scroll past.

Examples of strong visual hooks:

- child throws a toy
- child suddenly drops to the floor
- child screams and flails hands
- child runs away from mom
- child covers ears
- child hides under a table
- child pushes mom or fights
- child throws things around
- child has a meltdown in a store
- mom stands confused while people around look at them
- child jumps, laughs, and cannot stop
- child switches sharply from calm to chaos
- child sits shut down and does not respond
- mom tries to talk, but the child does not hear her

Important:

- the first 5 seconds must contain action, emotion, conflict, or unusual behavior

Does not qualify as a hook:

- child calmly playing
- mom simply smiling
- pretty room shot
- app screen without context
- abstract cards without a real-life situation

Formula:

- first chaos / unusual behavior / strong emotion
- then explanation and solution

The render should open with the problem scene, not with a cute neutral child shot.

## Visual Match Rule

Every voiceover phrase must have visual confirmation.

Example:

Voiceover:

`Я не знала що робити`

Frame:

- mom sitting confused

Not:

- child playing

## Emotional Ladder

The video should move through this path:

- Problem
- Recognition
- Hope
- Solution
- Result

## Required Visual Blocks

### 1. Real Problem

Minimum 20% of the video.

Use footage such as:

- meltdown
- tears
- conflict
- overstimulation
- exhausted mom

### 2. Mom

Minimum 20% of the video.

Show:

- emotions
- shame
- tiredness
- helplessness
- relief

Winning creatives often sell through the mom's emotional state.

### 3. Child

Minimum 30% of the video.

Show:

- emotions
- overstimulation
- tiredness
- state shift

### 4. Solution

Minimum 20% of the video.

Show:

- neuro exercise
- card
- app
- quiz
- recommendation

Do not just talk about the solution.

Show it.

### 5. Result

Minimum 10% of the video.

Use footage such as:

- calm child
- smile
- connection
- hugs
- playing together

## Social Proof

Whenever possible, include at least one element per video:

- review
- comment
- another mom
- screenshot
- message

A strong recurring idea is:

`Я не одна така`

Rule:

- do not use social proof as a random overlay pasted over unrelated footage
- prefer native review footage, real message screens, or deliberate social proof inserts as their own visual beat

## Overlay Requirements

Format:

- one idea = one overlay

Length:

- 2-6 words

Examples:

- `До вечора все змінюється`
- `Я вже все перепробувала`
- `Відволікати не працює`
- `Спочатку тіло`
- `Потім слова`
- `Через кілька днів`

## Show More Often

The matrix indicates these win more often:

- evening
- overstimulation
- mom
- fast result
- personal recommendation
- emotional cards
- calm without yelling

## Ukraine Market Casting Rule

These creatives are for the Ukrainian market.

Use casting, family context, interiors, and everyday situations that feel believable for this audience.

Avoid footage that breaks local audience realism for no strategic reason.

If a stock clip feels visually out of market, replace it.

## Repetition Rule

Do not repeat the same clip, same camera setup, or near-identical motion pattern unless there is a very clear reason.

If a shot is reused, it should be a deliberate callback, not a filler repeat.

If there is any doubt, prefer a new shot.

## Speed Rule

The montage should feel faster than explanatory content.

Default shot duration target:

- 1.0-2.2 seconds for most beats
- up to 2.5 seconds only when the scene needs readability for product, proof, or result

If the video starts to feel static, shorten the shot.

## Show Less Often

These tend to lose:

- abstract brain development
- generic explanations
- long neuropsychology lectures

## Winning Visual Concepts

- Quiz + personal recommendation
- Fast result from exercises
- Emotional cards as a tool
- Body first, words later
- Mom's shame
- Calm without yelling

## Final Render Check

Before every render, ask:

1. Is there a strong visual hook in the first 3 seconds?
2. Is there a mom in frame?
3. Is there a real problem?
4. Is there a visible solution?
5. Is there a visible result?

If any one of these blocks is missing, the video is incomplete.
